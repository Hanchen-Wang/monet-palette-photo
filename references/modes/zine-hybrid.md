# Zine Hybrid

Use when the user explicitly asks for a mixed photo, paint, paper, or restrained line-art composition.

## Source contract

- Source role: truthful photographic anchor and factual source for painted and drawn extensions.
- Source pixels in final: partial and required.
- Preserve: one recognizable photo region containing the semantic anchor.
- Transform: one source-derived light, atmosphere, reflection, or color field into Monet-inspired paint.
- Optional support: one restrained Line or Paper treatment.

## Composition

- Default strength: M2.
- Default presentation profile: `sparse_social`.
- Use an asymmetric photo anchor plus an expansive but low-density paint field, or let one source axis cross photo and paint.
- Extract one primary source grammar—prefer cutout, silhouette, contour, field, or rhythm—and allow at most one restrained supporting grammar.
- Keep one dominant active cluster and deliberate quiet paper or low-information fields; do not distribute equally weighted collage fragments across the frame.
- Use only one primary boundary strategy. Torn Field is allowed here; Brush Intrusion, Dissolve, Light Bridge, and Reflection Bridge remain valid.
- Keep paper flat and scanned rather than layered into a three-dimensional scrapbook.
- Typography is optional and subordinate: one exact two-to-four-word title or one short caption.

## Hierarchy

1. Semantic photo anchor.
2. Monet-inspired paint event.
3. Quiet paper or line support.
4. Optional micro-type.

Paint remains the art-direction focus; zine devices must not turn the work into a generic poster template.

## Hard avoids

- barcode, fake coordinates, archive numbers, random captions, or magazine filler;
- several paper tears, stickers, tape, shadows, and type systems competing at once;
- high-contrast line art overpowering the paint;
- arbitrary bright rectangles or dots unrelated to the source;
- copying example subjects, palettes, or layouts.
- filling every quiet area with scraps, brush marks, captions, or decorative symbols.
