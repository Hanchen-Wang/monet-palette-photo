# Subject Monetization

Use when the user wants the primary subject painted and the environment reduced to a quiet non-photographic support.

## Source contract

- Source role: factual anchor for subject identity, pose, silhouette, proportions, and essential context.
- Source pixels in final: none by default.
- Monet zone: the named primary subject and only the supporting fragments needed to ground it.
- Background: Simplify, Line, or Paper; choose one.
- Preserve semantically: subject identity and its key relationship to the environment.

## Analysis focus

Identify:

- the subject's most recognizable contours and internal landmarks;
- where light changes the subject's local color;
- which edges need L1 or L2 stability for recognition;
- one or two supporting context cues;
- what background information can disappear.

## Composition and strength

- Default strength: M3.
- Default presentation profile: `sparse_social`.
- Let brush direction follow anatomy, folds, petals, surface planes, or the subject's movement.
- Keep the background lower in contrast, chroma, detail, and texture.
- If the subject is a person, preserve face and hands at a lower local strength unless the user explicitly prioritizes painterly dissolution.

## Hard avoids

- high-contrast comic linework behind the painted subject;
- a detailed photographic-looking background competing with the subject;
- generic paint splashes detached from the silhouette or light;
- replacement of the actual subject with a period costume or historical figure.
