# Distilled Monet

Use when the user wants no photographic material in the result and welcomes a freer reconstruction of the source's semantic nucleus, light relationship, color relationship, and surface rhythm.

## Source contract

- Source role: semantic, structural, light, and color evidence only.
- Source pixels in final: none.
- Preserve: two to four anchors such as subject relationship, dominant axis, light event, reflection logic, or characteristic palette tension.
- Recompose: scale, interval, crop, repetition, edge certainty, and surface rhythm may change when they strengthen the source-derived proposition.
- Do not preserve the original composition by default.

## Analysis focus

Resolve:

- semantic nucleus;
- one central perceptual tension;
- dominant light and temperature relation;
- two to four source anchors;
- one primary surface rhythm;
- which factual details should disappear.

## Composition and strength

- Default strength: M4.
- Default presentation profile: `sparse_social`.
- Allow broad or sweeping strokes, reduced spatial certainty, and nonliteral but source-responsive color.
- Keep one readable anchor so the result feels distilled from this source rather than generated from a generic Monet prompt.
- When `sparse_social` is active, compress the scene into one dominant field or silhouette relationship plus at most two supporting marks. Quiet areas remain fully rendered color or paper-like surface, never exposed photography.
- Use no photography, tracing, or photorealistic fragments.

## Hard avoids

- a literal filtered copy of the original composition;
- generic abstract color fields with no traceable source relation;
- multiple unrelated metaphors or motifs;
- automatic water-lily, bridge, garden, or sunset symbolism.
