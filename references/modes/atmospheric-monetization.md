# Atmospheric Monetization

This is the signature selective mode. Use when the user wants the semantic anchor to remain photographic while light, air, reflection, water, weather, foliage shimmer, or unstable peripheral edges become painted.

## Source contract

- Source role: preserved factual anchor plus evidence for painted zones.
- Source pixels in final: partial and required.
- Preserve by default: faces, hands, body identity, important objects, logos, stable architecture, and the main spatial relationship.
- Monet zones: only the validated primary and secondary zones.
- Tool requirement: reference-image editing with regional control, a mask, or a deterministic composite path.

## Strategies

### Light-triggered

Transform areas touched by the dominant light and its reflected spill. Keep unlit anchor regions photographic unless the mask explicitly includes them. Let the light boundary determine the transition.

### Reflection-triggered

Keep the real subject photographic and paint the reflection or reflective environment. Align color rhythm and distortion to the real reflection axis; do not invent water or glass.

### Atmosphere-led

Paint fog, haze, rain, distant foliage, sky diffusion, or air perspective. Preserve nearer stable anchors and let edge loss increase with distance or atmospheric interference.

## Composition and strength

- Default strength: M2.
- Default presentation profile: `immersive`.
- The painted event should become the main perceptual focus without erasing the photographic anchor.
- Prefer Brush Intrusion, Dissolve, Light Bridge, or Reflection Bridge. Never use a hard digital split.
- A secondary Monet zone must share the same light, atmosphere, or reflection event.

## Hard avoids

- painting outside the transformation mask;
- global restyling of preserved photo zones;
- blurred faces or altered identity caused by spill;
- painted light that contradicts the observed direction;
- invented reflections or weather.
